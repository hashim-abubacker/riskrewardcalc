# Understanding Risk Reward Ratios in Trading

When you start trading, whether it's Forex, Crypto, or Stocks, you will hear one phrase repeated constantly: **Risk Management**. The core of risk management is the Risk/Reward Ratio (RRR).

## What is a Risk/Reward Ratio?

The risk/reward ratio measures how much your potential reward is, for every dollar you risk.

For example, if you risk $10 on a trade (by setting a Stop Loss) to make $30 (by setting a Take Profit), your Risk/Reward ratio is 1:3.

- **Risk:** $10
- **Reward:** $30
- **Ratio:** 1:3

## Why does it matter?

Having a positive risk-reward ratio means you don't need to win every trade to be profitable.

If your RRR is 1:3, you can lose 7 out of 10 trades and *still break even*!
- 7 losses at $10 each = -$70
- 3 wins at $30 each = +$90
- **Net Profit:** +$20
